How to Enroll

To enroll in FamilyAI SMS messaging, text JOIN to (435) 466-5977.

By enrolling, you agree to receive FamilyAI SMS messages, including reminders, calendar updates, planning assistance, and requested communications.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Enrollment Process

After texting JOIN to (435) 466-5977, FamilyAI sends the required SMS disclosures and asks you to confirm enrollment by replying YES. After you reply YES, FamilyAI confirms your enrollment in FamilyAI SMS messaging.
